# Tesla AI Product Manager

## Role Summary
Drive AI products for Tesla's Autopilot, Optimus, and Dojo-powered solutions.

## Key Responsibilities
- Oversee real-world AI deployment in autonomous vehicles/robotics.
- Optimize model performance for edge devices.
- Ensure regulatory compliance for AI in mobility.

## Required Skills
### Technical
- Expertise in computer vision (PyTorch, Tesla's HydraNet).
- Experience with Dojo and edge AI optimization.

### Business
- Automotive/robotics industry knowledge.
- Regulatory landscape for autonomous systems.

## Qualifications
- 5+ years in AI product management, preferably in automotive.

## AI-Specific Requirements
- Understanding of sensor fusion and real-time AI inference.
