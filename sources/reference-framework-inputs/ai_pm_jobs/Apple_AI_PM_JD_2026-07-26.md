# Apple AI Product Manager

## Role Summary
Shape the future of AI-powered consumer products at Apple, including Siri, on-device ML, and privacy-focused AI solutions.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define and execute AI product strategies for Apple's ecosystem.
- Collaborate with hardware and software teams to optimize on-device ML.
- Ensure user privacy and data security in AI applications.
- Lead A/B testing and performance tuning of AI features.

## Required Skills
### Technical
- Proficiency in ML frameworks: Core ML, TensorFlow Lite.
- Cloud platforms: Apple's private cloud or AWS/GCP.
- Data pipelines: Apache Airflow, Apple's internal tools.
- Tools: MLflow, Kubeflow, Apple's MLX.

### Business
- Strong UX/UI sensibilities for consumer-facing AI.
- Ability to work in a highly collaborative, cross-functional environment.
- Experience in agile or iterative product development.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's in AI, Computer Science, or related field.
- 4+ years of product management, with 2+ years in AI/ML.

## AI-Specific Requirements
- Expertise in on-device ML and edge computing.
- Understanding of differential privacy and federated learning.
