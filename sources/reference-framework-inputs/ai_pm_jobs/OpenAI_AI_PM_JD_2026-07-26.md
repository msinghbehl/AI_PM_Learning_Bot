# OpenAI AI Product Manager

## Role Summary
Lead the development and deployment of AI products leveraging OpenAI's GPT ecosystem, ensuring alignment with ethical AI principles and business objectives.

## Key Responsibilities
- Drive the AI product lifecycle from ideation to deployment for OpenAI's GPT-based products.
- Collaborate with research teams to translate cutting-edge AI advancements into scalable products.
- Ensure ethical AI deployment, focusing on safety, fairness, and transparency.
- Define and track KPIs for AI product performance and user adoption.

## Required Skills
### Technical
- Proficiency in OpenAI's GPT ecosystem (GPT-4, ChatGPT, API integrations).
- Experience with AI model deployment, fine-tuning, and monitoring.
- Familiarity with AI ethics frameworks and safety protocols.

### Business
- Strong stakeholder management across engineering, research, and business teams.
- Ability to align AI product roadmaps with business goals.
- Experience in go-to-market strategies for AI products.

## Qualifications
- 5+ years in AI product management or related roles.
- Background in computer science, AI, or a technical field.
- Proven track record of launching AI-driven products.

## AI-Specific Requirements
- Deep understanding of generative AI and its applications.
- Experience with OpenAI's API and developer ecosystem.
- Knowledge of AI safety and alignment research.
