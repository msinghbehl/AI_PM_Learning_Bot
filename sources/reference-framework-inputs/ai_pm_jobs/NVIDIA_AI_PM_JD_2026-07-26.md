# NVIDIA AI Product Manager

## Role Summary
Drive the development of AI and GPU-accelerated products at NVIDIA, focusing on deep learning, autonomous systems, and high-performance computing.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead AI product development for GPU-accelerated applications.
- Work with research teams to commercialize AI breakthroughs.
- Ensure scalability and performance of AI models on NVIDIA hardware.
- Advocate for ethical AI in autonomous systems and healthcare.

## Required Skills
### Technical
- Expertise in ML frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, CUDA.
- Cloud platforms: AWS, Azure, or NVIDIA DGX.
- Data pipelines: Apache Kafka, NVIDIA RAPIDS.
- Tools: MLflow, Kubeflow, Triton Inference Server.

### Business
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to align AI products with market needs.
- Experience in B2B or B2C AI product launches.

## Qualifications
- Advanced degree in AI, Computer Science, or Engineering.
- 5+ years in product management, with 3+ years in AI/ML.

## AI-Specific Requirements
- Knowledge of model optimization for GPU architectures.
- Familiarity with AI ethics and compliance standards.
