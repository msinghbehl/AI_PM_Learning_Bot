# DeepMind AI Product Manager

## Role Summary
Lead AI product development for DeepMind's research-driven initiatives, focusing on healthcare, robotics, and AGI alignment.

## Key Responsibilities
- Translate cutting-edge AI research into scalable products.
- Collaborate with ML researchers on reinforcement learning and multimodal models.
- Ensure ethical deployment of AI in sensitive domains (e.g., healthcare).

## Required Skills
### Technical
- Expertise in JAX, TensorFlow, and reinforcement learning frameworks.
- Experience with large-scale distributed training.
- Knowledge of AI safety and interpretability tools.

### Business
- Ability to bridge research and commercial applications.
- Stakeholder management in academia/enterprise.

## Qualifications
- PhD in AI/ML or 5+ years in AI product management.
- Published research in AI/ML (preferred).

## AI-Specific Requirements
- Familiarity with AlphaFold, Gemini, or other DeepMind breakthroughs.
