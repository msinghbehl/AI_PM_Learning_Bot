# Meta AI Product Manager

## Role Summary
Lead the development and deployment of AI-driven products at Meta, focusing on social media, AR/VR, and digital advertising. Ensure ethical AI practices and seamless integration of ML models into user-facing applications.

## Key Responsibilities
- Oversee the AI product lifecycle from ideation to deployment.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define AI product roadmaps.
- Ensure ethical AI practices, including bias mitigation and transparency.
- Monitor model performance and iterate based on user feedback.

## Required Skills
### Technical
- Proficiency in ML frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow.
- Experience with cloud platforms: AWS, GCP, or Meta's internal infrastructure.
- Familiarity with data pipelines: Apache Beam, Spark.
- Tools: MLflow for model tracking, Kubeflow for orchestration.

### Business
- Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
- Ability to translate business needs into technical requirements.
- Experience in agile product development.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, AI, or related field.
- 5+ years of product management experience, with 2+ years in AI/ML.

## AI-Specific Requirements
- Deep understanding of evaluation metrics (e.g., precision, recall, F1).
- Experience with deploying models in production environments.
