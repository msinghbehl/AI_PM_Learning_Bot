Nvidia, OpenAI, and Anthropic all share one thing in PM interviews: they ask technical questions.

One expectation top AI companies have of their PMs is that they want you to walk in and win the respect of your engineering and research counterparts.

With the rise of technical AI content like Andrej Karpathy's "Build ChatGPT in 3 hours" content, the bar has risen on what technical AI folks expect out of non-technical ones, especially PMs.

So I set about putting together the most common questions reported and put them into 8 categories, so they're easier to prepare.

## Category 1: Most Common

- Explain how Transformers Work?
- What is attention?

These types of questions are the depth filter, and if you freeze in response, that's not a good sign. It's not so much that a good response will wow them. But a bad response will be a red flag. You probably know the answers, but practice saying them consistently. And if you're going for a model or technical PM role, study the following categories, too.

## Category 2: Tools, Agents & MCP

- How does MCP compare to one-off API integrations?
- What makes something an agent vs a chatbot?

Resource: https://lnkd.in/eeey5Cxr

## Category 3: Orchestration & Routing

- What is model routing and why does it matter?
- How does routing affect unit economics at millions of calls?

A PM who can explain why routing 60% of traffic to a cheaper model changes the margin story gets scored senior.

## Category 4: LLM Fundamentals

- What's the difference between pre-training and post-training?
- Why isn't an LLM a database you query?

If your mental model is "the model looks things up," everything downstream falls apart. Hallucination, RAG… None of it makes sense until you internalize the model predicts.
